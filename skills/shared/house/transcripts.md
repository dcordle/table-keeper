# Transcripts

How to wrap up a session from a recording's transcript. A 3 to 4 hour
session runs long, so work in passes and never invent what the transcript
does not show.

## Getting the transcript

Take it from where the profile's `Transcripts` field says, or from the
file the GM gives in chat. Read it in parts of about half an hour of
play each, in order, rather than all at once.

## Pass 1: who is speaking

Map each speaker label to a person before reading for events. Use the
session's roster from the record, names the speakers use for each other,
and who narrates (the GM). Write the map first. A speaker you cannot place
stays unplaced, and what they say is marked unclear rather than given to
someone.

## Pass 2: what happened

For each part, keep short running notes, in play order:

- where the group went and what they did there;
- who they met, and what each NPC told them or kept back;
- clues found, matched to the prep's must-learn facts where there was
  prep, and any optional archive clue;
- rumors heard;
- fights: who, how they ended, anyone downed, curses, conditions;
- loot, pay and promises made;
- rulings the GM made at the table, flagged for review;
- anything the campaign's own debrief asks for.

Skip table talk that is not play, except rulings and decisions about the
game. Where crosstalk or a gap makes an event uncertain, mark it unclear.

## Pass 3: the wrap-up

From the notes, give:

- a short recap in the profile's player-facing voice;
- the result: success, partial, deal, failure or withdrawal;
- the after-session order from `prep-core.md`, naming the doc or note
  each change belongs in;
- the unclear points as a short list of questions for the GM.

Quote the transcript only in short pieces. Write changes to the record
only as the profile's `Record` mode allows: in Project-docs mode, after
the GM confirms.
