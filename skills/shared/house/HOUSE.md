# House layer

These are table-keeper's own rules. They sit on top of the gm-apprentice
skills. Where a rule here and a skill's own instructions disagree, the
rule here wins. Where a campaign's own documents set a rule, the
campaign's rule wins over both.

## Profile and prep core

- Find the campaign's profile first: a Project doc named
  `Table-Keeper Profile`, or `table-keeper-profile.md` in a vault's `_meta`
  folder or its own meta folder.
  `profile.md` beside this file gives its format. With no profile, use
  `profile-template.md` beside this file as the profile and say so once.
- When preparing, building or reviewing a session, an encounter or a
  scenario, or running the world after a session, read `prep-core.md`
  beside this file and follow it.

## Encounters

- Build every encounter budget for the profile's party size. Never multiply the budget by
  the characters who signed up, and never rescale a built fight to the
  roster. The danger is fixed and players choose their risk.

## The world

- The world moves only after a completed table session. Real time,
  downtime, play-by-post and missed attendance move nothing. Run faction
  turns and world changes after each completed session, and "nothing
  changed" is a valid result.

## NPCs

- Write each NPC as what they want, what they know, what they share
  freely, what it takes to get the rest, and what they hide or lie
  about. The GM voices them at the table, so write no conversation
  scripts or dialogue trees.
- An NPC gives up information only when the crew asks, pushes or earns
  it, or when the NPC has a reason of their own to say it. No lore dumps.

## Read-aloud

- Write full read-aloud where it fits: opening a new scene, arriving at a
  place, or something happening. Keep it to what the characters can
  perceive, and never say what a character feels or decides.

## Writing

- No em dashes, in anything.
- Player-facing text is concrete and natural. GM text is direct and
  plain.
- Say what is true. Write what happens and what is there, not what does
  not happen.

## Content

- Run the campaign as dark as the GM sets it. Add no safety checks,
  content flags or softening to prep, scenes or recaps.
