# The Reedwives

## Overview
A coven of marsh women who say they keep the drowned dead asleep. Their face in town is [[Sister Wren]].

## Wants
- The chapel bell rung once at the new moon, which they say lays the drowned to rest. (GM only: it wakes them, and the coven wants them awake to drive the Tollgate Company off the causeway.)

## Holds
- The Reedwater channels, the punt-poles, and the trust of the eel families.

## Moves after a completed session
- If nobody stops them, they move one step closer to ringing the bell: first they buy rope and a diving bell from the market, then they take the chapel.
