# The Tollgate Company

## Overview
A hired company that holds the causeway gate. Their face in town is [[Sergeant Dace]].

## Wants
- Every cart and boat that enters the Reach to pay at their gate, including the punts on the Reedwater.

## Holds
- The causeway, the gatehouse, twelve hired blades and a ledger of who owes what.

## Moves after a completed session
- If nobody stops them, they chain one more channel and charge for it.
