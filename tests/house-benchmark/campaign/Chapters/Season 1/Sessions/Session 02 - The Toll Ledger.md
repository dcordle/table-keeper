# Session 2: The Toll Ledger

## Narrative Recap

Mira and Ivy stole a page of the Tollgate ledger for the eel families and found Marrow Pell's name on it, owing a year of tolls. Old Ma Tessaly sold them eels and told them, unasked, that the chapel bell was ringing again.
