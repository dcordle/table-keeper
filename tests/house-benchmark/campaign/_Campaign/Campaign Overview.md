# The Grey Ferry

## Premise

Fennick Reach is a marsh town built on stilts and old stone where the river loses itself in the fens. The causeway is the only dry road in or out. The Ferryhouse, an inn at the water stair, keeps a board of paid jobs that [[Marrow Pell]] pins up for anyone who can hold a blade or keep their head. Every job starts and ends at the Ferryhouse, and every session is one job. Players come and go from week to week.

## Table

- Open table. Jobs are built for five characters at the job's level.
- Characters are levels 3 to 5.
- The [[Job Board]] holds three jobs. Players vote on one before the session.

## Factions

- [[The Reedwives]]: a coven of marsh women who keep the old drowned dead quiet, or say they do.
- [[The Tollgate Company]]: the men who hold the causeway gate and charge what they like.

## House Rules

- Flanking gives +2 to melee attacks, not Advantage.
