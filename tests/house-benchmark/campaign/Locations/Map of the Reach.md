# Map of the Reach

## Overview
**Type:** Region map, nodes and danger words
**Associated NPCs:** [[Marrow Pell]], [[Old Ma Tessaly]], [[Sergeant Dace]], [[Sister Wren]]

## Nodes

| Node | Danger word | Links | Holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Ferryhouse | Safe | Market Stair | The inn and the job board |
| Market Stair | Quiet | The Ferryhouse, Old Causeway, Reedwater | Eel stalls, [[Old Ma Tessaly]] |
| Old Causeway | Uneasy | Market Stair, Tollgate | The only dry road; Tollgate Company patrols |
| Tollgate | Watched | Old Causeway | The gatehouse of [[The Tollgate Company]] |
| Reedwater | Dangerous | Market Stair, Drowned Chapel | Reed channels, punts only |
| Drowned Chapel | Deadly | Reedwater | A chapel sunk to its bell tower; the bell is under the water |

## Travel complications (d6, only when travel matters, never homeward)

- **Quiet:** 1 a toll man asks for coin; 2 to 6 nothing.
- **Uneasy:** 1 a patrol searches packs; 2 fog, the way is lost for an hour; 3 to 6 nothing.
- **Dangerous:** 1 a swarm of leeches in the punt; 2 a sunk log holes the punt; 3 fog; 4 to 6 nothing.
- **Deadly:** 1 the drowned stir and two zombies rise from the shallows; 2 the punt grounds and the crew wades; 3 a cold that costs one Hit Die; 4 to 6 nothing.
