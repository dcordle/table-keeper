## What you need to decide first

1. **What rings the bell?** The draft has the drowned sexton, risen as a Wight, ringing it by hand from the flooded belfry. The other two seeds are a Sea Hag luring punt-boys, or the drowned climbing the tower on their own.
2. **Who is the missing punt-boy?** The draft makes him Old Ma Tessaly's grandson, alive in an air pocket under the belfry roof. That rescue is the hopeful beat your profile asks for.
3. **Who cut the rope forty years ago, and will Ma Tessaly say so?** Her note records that she knows, but not who it was. The draft says the sexton did it.
4. **Is the new moon during this job, and do the Reedwives have their diving bell yet?** The draft says no to both. Their faction note puts the diving bell in the same step as the rope, but Session 2 only records the rope.
5. **What is Pell's interest?** The draft says he just wants quiet water. The other seed is that the Company has offered to forgive his debt if the chapel water can be chained.

## Main fight check (draft, first seed)

The fight is the drowned sexton (Wight, 700 XP), two drowned churchwardens (Ghast, 450 XP each) and six drowned parishioners (Zombie, 50 XP each). That totals 1,900 XP.

- For five level 4 characters, Moderate is 1,875 and High is 2,500. At 1,900 it sits just inside your band and is built once, not scaled to the roster.
- **Low end, level 3 (risk note):** for a level 3 party this is close to High (2,000). The Ghast claw paralyzes on a DC 10 CON save, and melee hits on a paralyzed character are crits. Two Ghasts focusing one level 3 PC is the swing risk, made worse by deep water and bad footing.
- **High end, level 5:** for a level 5 party, 1,900 XP is below Low (2,500). A table of level 5s will find it easy unless the water and the dark do some of the work.
- The Deadly travel line can add two Zombies on the way in. Those count as an extra fight outside the band.

Stat notes, for your yes before I write them as creature notes (SRD 5.2, hit points as written):

- **Drowned Sexton (Wight):** AC 14, HP 82, Init +4. Multiattack twice with Necrotic Sword (+4, 1d8+2 slashing plus 1d8 necrotic). Life Drain at DC 13 CON for 1d8+2 necrotic. Resists necrotic, Sunlight Sensitivity, 700 XP.
- **Drowned Churchwarden (Ghast) x2:** AC 13, HP 36, Init +3. Bite +5 for 1d8+3 piercing plus 2d8 necrotic. Claw +5 for 2d6+3 slashing, with a DC 10 CON save against Paralyzed for 1 turn. Stench DC 10, 450 XP each.
- **Drowned Parishioner (Zombie) x6:** AC 8, HP 15, speed 20 ft. Slam +3 for 1d8+1. Undead Fortitude, 50 XP each.

## Session Intent

The table voted for [[Job Board]]'s "The Bell Under the Water": [[Marrow Pell]] pays to learn what rings the drowned chapel's bell and to stop it. Levels 3 to 5, built at level 4.

## Session Overview

- The eel families hear the [[Drowned Chapel]] bell ringing under the water at night, though its rope rotted away forty years ago.
- Two punt-boys went out on Tuesday to look; one came back.
