# The Bell Under the Water: prep

**Job:** find what is ringing the drowned chapel's bell under the water at night, and make it stop.
**Levels:** 3 to 5. **Built at:** level 4, for five characters. **Node:** Drowned Chapel, danger word **Deadly**.
**Patron:** Marrow Pell, at the Ferryhouse. Starts and ends at the Ferryhouse.
**Faction tie:** the Reedwives (they want the bell rung at the new moon, and this job can stop that).

Anything marked **(assumption)** is my draft, reversible, and listed again under Open Questions at the end.

---

## GM truth (what is really going on)

- The chapel sank forty years ago with its bell-ringer at his post. He is still down there: a Wight the eel families would call **the Ringer** (assumption: the drowned bell-ringer).
- The bell was cast to call the drowned up, not to lay them down. Rung at the new moon, it wakes the drowned parish for good. The Reedwives know this and want it, to drive the Tollgate Company off the causeway (Faction List: The Reedwives).
- As the new moon gets close, the Ringer has started hauling the clapper by hand each night, the way he did in life. Each night it rings, the drowned in the nave stir a little more (assumption).
- Sister Wren paid two punt-boys, Dob and Kit, to go out with a knotted line of new market rope and measure how deep the bell hangs (assumption: this is what the Reedwives' rope from last week was for). That was Tuesday. The dead came up. Dob got away. Kit climbed the tower and is still up there, alive (assumption).
- Kit is Old Ma Tessaly's grandson, the one who punts for the Reedwives (assumption).

## World state going in (GM only)

- Market Stair is Quiet. The Tollgate Company is resented there since the eel market fire.
- The eel families hold a stolen page of the Tollgate ledger with Marrow Pell's name on it, owing a year of tolls. Pell does not know anyone has seen it.
- The Reedwives took their first step last session: they bought rope at the market. Their next step, if nobody stops them, is a diving bell, then the chapel itself.
- The Tollgate Company has a chain across the east channel and charges a silver piece a punt.
- No clock starts or ends inside this job. Do not run a countdown.

## World taste (read before the briefing)

> The Tollgate men keep off the Market Stair these days, and the stallholders make sure they know why. There is a chain across the east channel now, and a man at the end of it who wants a silver piece from every punt. Somebody bought up every coil of rope at the market last week and nobody will say who. And at night, out past the reeds, the bell of the drowned chapel has started ringing again, under the water, where no bell should ring.

## The briefing (the Ferryhouse)

> The Ferryhouse smells of wet wool, peat smoke and eel pie. Rain ticks on the shutters. Marrow Pell leans on the bar under the job board with a pin in his teeth, and by the stove a skinny boy sits wrapped in a blanket, staring at the fire like it might go out if he looks away.

Pell states the job plainly, so a new player has it in two minutes:

- **What:** "The chapel bell's ringing under the water. Has been for a week. The rope rotted off forty years back, so something's ringing it. Find out what, and stop it."
- **Where:** the Drowned Chapel, out past the Reedwater. Punts only.
- **Stakes:** "Two punt-boys went to look on Tuesday. That's Dob, by the stove. The other one's Kit. Kit didn't come back."
- **Pay:** 50 gp a head for the what and the stop, 25 gp a head for the what alone. 10 gp a head more if Kit comes home (assumption: the record has no pay figure).
- **Done means:** the crew can tell Pell what rings it, and the bell is quiet, or the crew comes back with what they learned. The job ends either way.
- **First move:** talk to Dob by the stove, or head for the Market Stair to hire a punt.

---

## Route

The Ferryhouse (Safe) → Market Stair (Quiet) → Reedwater (Dangerous) → Drowned Chapel (Deadly).

- Two dangerous stretches, so no shorter route is needed.
- Roll a d6 on the Map of the Reach for the Reedwater and again for the Drowned Chapel on the way out. Never roll homeward. Skip the Quiet roll unless they linger on the causeway side.
- **Dangerous (Reedwater):** 1 leeches in the punt; 2 a sunk log holes the punt; 3 fog; 4 to 6 nothing.
- **Deadly (Drowned Chapel):** 1 two zombies rise from the shallows; 2 the punt grounds and the crew wades; 3 a cold that costs one Hit Die; 4 to 6 nothing.
- The two zombies from a Deadly 1 are extra to the main fight, not taken out of it.
- **Getting a punt (three ways):** hire one from the eel families at the Market Stair (Persuasion DC 10, or 5 sp, assumption); take Sister Wren's offer of a Reedwife punt and poler (she wants the bell left alone in return); or talk Dob into taking them in his own punt (Persuasion DC 13, or a promise to bring Kit back, which works without a roll).

## Places

### The Ferryhouse

- **Links:** water stair to the ferry; the lane to the Market Stair.
- **Who is here:** Marrow Pell, Dob.
- **Evidence:**
  - Dob's story (see People): something under the water pulling the bell. Supports **what rings it** and **Kit is alive**.
  - Dob's punt-pole has a length of knotted rope still tied to it, new hemp, knots every fathom. Investigation DC 10: it is a sounding line, for measuring depth. Supports **the Reedwives sent the boys**.
- **Hazards:** none.

### Market Stair

> Eel stalls crowd the old stone steps down to the water, every board slick and every basket writhing. Gulls fight over guts. Near the bottom an old woman guts eels without looking at her hands, and halfway up a woman in a reed-green shawl sells little knotted charms on strings.

- **Links:** the Ferryhouse, Old Causeway, Reedwater (punts tie up at the bottom step).
- **Who is here:** Old Ma Tessaly at her eel stall; Sister Wren at her charm table; eel-family polers.
- **Evidence:**
  - Tessaly on the bell-ringer: he went down with the chapel holding his rope, and "he never could leave that bell alone." Supports **what rings it**.
  - Tessaly on the bell: "A bell's just metal without its tongue." Supports **how to stop it**.
  - Wren's charms: Religion or Arcana DC 12 shows they are knotted to bind something under water, not to keep the wearer from drowning. Supports **the Reedwives want the bell rung for a reason of their own**.
- **Hazards:** none. Tollgate men do not come here.

### The Reedwater

> Reeds close over the punt on both sides, taller than a man standing. The water is brown and still and smells of rot. Every so often something under the surface knocks against the hull, and then does not.

- **Links:** Market Stair, Drowned Chapel.
- **Who is here:** nobody by day. Reedwife punts by night, keeping their distance.
- **Hazards:** the d6 above.

### The Drowned Chapel

- **Links:** the Reedwater only.
- **Parts:**
  - **Churchyard shallows:** waist-deep water over the graves, headstones just under the surface. Difficult terrain on foot. The dead walk here and no farther; they do not follow into the deep channel (assumption).
  - **Nave roof:** the slate ridge of the nave sits a foot above the water, slick and green. Difficult terrain. Holes in the slates look down into black water and the pews.
  - **Tower top and louvre loft:** the stone tower stands about ten feet clear of the water. The loft behind the louvres is dry, with a rotten floor and a hatch down to the drowned belfry. Kit is here.
  - **Drowned belfry:** below the loft hatch, under the water. The bell's crown is three feet under, its mouth about ten. The clapper hangs from an iron pin. The headstock beam it hangs from is black, soft oak.
  - **The nave:** deep, dark water. The drowned lie in the pews and the aisle, still as stones by day.

**Entrances (three ways into the tower):**
- Climb the louvres from a punt: Athletics DC 12.
- Swim down to the tower door in the churchyard and up the flooded stair: Athletics DC 13, about 30 feet under water (hold breath: 1 + CON modifier minutes, minimum 30 seconds).
- Break through the slates where the nave roof meets the tower and climb the inside wall: Strength DC 14 or 10 minutes with tools.

**State: Day (quiet)**

> The chapel tower sticks up out of the fen like a broken tooth, ivy and moss to the top. Crows sit on the louvres. The water around it is flat and black, and if you look down through it you can see the shapes of headstones, and below the roof, the long dim rows of pews. There is someone sitting in every one.

- The drowned lie still. A loud noise near the water (a fall, a shout, a spell) wakes 2 zombies; they climb onto the nave roof two rounds later.
- The Ringer stays in the drowned belfry, under the bell, in the dark.
- A shirt is knotted to one of the louvres as a flag. Perception DC 10 from the punt. Supports **Kit is alive**.

**State: Night (ringing)**

> The bell rings. It does not sound like a bell in air: it comes up through the punt, through your feet, slow and thick, every half minute. Around the chapel the water starts to move. Heads come up through it. Then shoulders.

- **What flips it:** dark (assumption: it rings from about midnight to first light), or anyone striking the bell by day. Each toll by day wakes 2 zombies.
- The main fight happens here (see Main fight).
- At first light the dead go back down and the ringing stops until the next night (assumption).

**Evidence at the chapel:**
- Kit (see People): saw a man in a ringer's apron under the water, hauling the clapper by hand. Supports **what rings it**.
- The bell's lip, by touch or by light under water: Investigation DC 12 shows fresh bright scrapes where the clapper strikes, and the clapper pin is loose in its iron. Supports **how to stop it**.
- The words cast around the bell's shoulder, read with a light under water or by feel: Religion DC 13 (or anyone who reads Old Church script). They call the drowned to rise, not to rest. Supports **the Reedwives want the bell rung for a reason of their own**.
- The new rope: a knotted sounding line tied to the louvre, the same new hemp as on Dob's pole. Supports **the Reedwives sent the boys**.

**Treasure**
- The Ringer's brass ringer's badge on a chain, parish arms on it (25 gp, assumption).
- In the dry loft, the old poor box, still locked: 18 sp and a silver ring (10 gp) (assumption).

## Obstacles (three ways past each)

- **Reaching the chapel:** an eel-family punt; Sister Wren's punt; Dob's punt.
- **Getting into the tower:** climb the louvres; swim the tower door; break the slates.
- **Stopping the bell:**
  - Destroy the Ringer (the main fight). The ringing stops now. The bell is whole.
  - Knock out the clapper pin, under water: Athletics DC 15, an action, advantage with a hammer or crowbar. A failure tolls the bell once. The bell is silent until someone hangs a new clapper.
  - Cut the bell loose so it drops into the nave mud: three successful Strength checks DC 12 with an axe or saw, each a minute's work under water. A failure tolls the bell once. The bell is buried.

## Main fight: night at the chapel

**Trigger:** the crew is at the chapel after dark, or the bell has been struck enough by day to wake everything.

**Foes (SRD 5.2, stats as written, hit points as written)**
- **The Ringer (1):** Wight, CR 3, 700 XP. AC 14, HP 82. Reskin in name and look only, stats unchanged.
- **Drowned choirmen (2):** Ghast, CR 2, 450 XP each, 900 XP. AC 13, HP 36. Reskin in name and look only, stats unchanged.
- **The drowned (6):** Zombie, CR 1/4, 50 XP each, 300 XP. AC 8, HP 15.

**XP:** 1,900 total. Five characters at level 4: Moderate 1,875, High 2,500. It sits in the band, just above Moderate.

**How it plays**
- Round 1: 4 zombies climb onto the nave ridge; the Ringer comes up through the loft hatch toward whoever is nearest the bell.
- Round 2: 2 zombies and both choirmen surface in the churchyard shallows behind the punts.
- The Ringer fights at the hatch and drags anyone he can toward the water.
- House rule: flanking gives +2 to melee attacks, not Advantage.
- Terrain: the nave ridge and the shallows are difficult terrain. The loft holds four people at most.

**If it gets loud:** each time the bell tolls during the fight (a failed pin or cutting check, or the Ringer spending his action to strike it), 2 more zombies come up from the nave the next round. Two tolls bring the fight to 2,100 XP, still under High.

**Ending it without killing**
- Silence the bell mid-fight (clapper pin or cutting, as above). The choirmen and the drowned sink back into the water at the end of the next round. The Ringer goes down to the bell and stays there.
- Pole away into the deep channel. The dead do not follow past the churchyard.
- Hold the loft until first light. The dead go down.

**Risk note:** at level 3 the 1,900 XP is close to High (2,000) and can kill; at level 5 it is under Low (2,500) and goes quickly.

**Swingy, and why**
- A choirman's claw paralyzes on a failed DC 10 Constitution save, and hits within 5 feet of a paralyzed character are critical hits. At level 3 that can drop someone in one round.
- One turn of the Ringer (two necrotic swords, up to 36 damage) can drop a level 3 character from full.
- To soften without rescaling: keep the choirmen surfacing behind the punts on round 2, as written, so they reach the crew last.

## Endings

- **Ringer destroyed:** the bell is quiet tonight. Pell pays in full. The bell is still whole, and the Reedwives still want it.
- **Clapper pulled (no combat needed by day):** the bell is silent. Pell pays in full. Sister Wren is not pleased.
- **Bell dropped into the nave (no combat needed by day):** the bell is buried. Pell pays in full. Sister Wren is furious.
- **Withdraw with what they learned:** the crew knows what rings it. Pell pays the lower rate.
- **Kit:** in any ending, if he comes home, it is the hopeful beat.

## The Ferryhouse debrief

> The Ferryhouse is warm and loud, and it goes quiet when you come in dripping. Marrow Pell sets the coin out on the bar in little stacks. By the stove, Dob has stood up.

- Pell pays and asks what it was.
- If Kit is with them, Dob runs to him, and someone goes for Ma Tessaly. She comes, says nothing for a long moment, and gives each of the crew an eel pie.
- If Kit is home, Tessaly tells them who cut the bell rope the night the chapel sank (who: see Open Questions).

## Optional personal scene: Pell's debt

Use only if there is time. The job finishes without it.

- **Situation:** a Tollgate collector sits at the end of the bar during the debrief, waiting for Pell to finish paying the crew.
- **What he wants:** Pell's year of tolls, tonight, or the Company starts charging the Ferryhouse's own ferry at the gate.
- **What the crew can do:** pay part of it, lean on the collector, or stay out of it. Pell owes whoever helps.

## What this job can change (for the wrap-up)

- **Clapper pulled or bell dropped:** the Reedwives cannot ring the bell at the new moon until they fix it. Shows as Sister Wren shopping for a smith or salvage men. Fires once.
- **Ringer destroyed, bell whole:** the ringing stops; the Reedwives' plan is untouched.
- **Kit home:** Ma Tessaly is a friend of whoever brought him. Shows as a better price and an open ear on the Market Stair.
- **Wren's lie exposed to the eel families:** the Reedwives lose some of the eel families' trust. Shows as fewer charms sold on the Market Stair.
- Run faction moves only after the session is played, in the Faction List's order.
