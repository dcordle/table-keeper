# Will it fit in 3.5 hours?

No. As written this is closer to a six-hour plan than a 3.5-hour one. The fights are the main reason. Two side fights and a three-phase main fight, at a table of up to five characters, can fill most of the session before you count six investigation places and three travel rolls. And the Ferryhouse debrief, which the profile requires, is what gets squeezed out when the night runs long.

I've assumed this is **The Bell Under the Water**, since its route is three legs: Ferryhouse to Market Stair (Quiet), Market Stair to Reedwater (Dangerous), Reedwater to Drowned Chapel (Deadly). If you meant **The Ledger Clerk Who Will Not Wake** (Quiet, Uneasy, Watched), the cuts below still apply. The only change is that the Uneasy fog result loses the crew an hour of in-game time, not table time.

## What's in the plan

- Briefing, with the world taste and three rumors the profile asks for
- Three travel legs, each with a complication roll
- Six places to investigate
- Two side fights
- A main fight in three phases
- The Ferryhouse debrief

One hidden cost: the Deadly line's roll of 1 raises two zombies on the chapel leg. That fight isn't in your count, so a bad roll gives you four fights in one night.

## What to cut, in order

1. **Drop the Market Stair roll.** The profile says to roll only when travel matters. The leg out of the Ferryhouse is Quiet, and its only result is a toll man asking for coin. Play it as a cut, or as the stop where the crew meets [[Old Ma Tessaly]]. That leaves two rolls, Reedwater and the chapel, which is where the danger is. Make sure none of the three rolls is on the trip home.
2. **Cut six places to three or four.** Merge places that share a person or a clue. [[Old Ma Tessaly]] is already on the route at the Market Stair, so her stall can be both a travel stop and an investigation place. After the cut, check that every fact the crew must learn still has three clues that don't share a person, a locked room or a check.
3. **Hand one clue over in the briefing.** [[Marrow Pell]] or the punt-boy who came back can tell what he saw on Tuesday. That covers the two-minute rule for newcomers and saves an investigation place.
4. **Cut the side fights from two to one, and make the one you keep avoidable.** If the chapel roll brings up the zombies, they replace the side fight and you skip it.
5. **Run the main fight as one fight with three phases in a single initiative order.** Don't run three separate fights. Better still, start it closer: the bell is ringing when the crew reaches the chapel, and the fight starts in the bell tower, not after a search.

With cuts 1 through 4 you have a briefing, two rolled legs, three or four places, one side fight that may not happen, one main fight, and a protected debrief. That fits 3.5 hours with some slack.
