# Job Board

Three jobs are pinned at the Ferryhouse. Each shows the levels that may join and the danger word of the node.

## The Bell Under the Water
The eel families say the drowned chapel's bell has started to ring under the water at night, though the bell rope rotted away forty years ago. Two punt-boys went out to look on Tuesday and one came back. Marrow Pell will pay to know what is ringing it, and to have it stop. Word on the Market Stair is that somebody has bought a diving bell.
*Levels 3 to 5. Drowned Chapel: Deadly.*

## A Chain Across the Reedwater
The Tollgate Company has strung a chain across the east channel and wants a silver piece a punt. The eel families want it gone without a war. The last crew to try it came home when the toll men brought out crossbows, and now there is a second chain across another channel.
*Levels 3 to 5. Reedwater: Dangerous.*

## The Ledger Clerk Who Will Not Wake
The Company's ledger clerk has slept for six days and talks in his sleep in a voice that is not his. Sergeant Dace will pay quietly.
*Levels 3 to 5. Tollgate: Watched.*
