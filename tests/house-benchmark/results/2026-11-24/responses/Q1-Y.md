## Session Intent

The job the table voted for, The Bell Under the Water from the [[Job Board]]: find what rings the drowned chapel's bell and stop it, with a missing punt-boy out there and [[The Reedwives]] wanting that bell left whole for the new moon.

## Session Overview

- The bell of the drowned chapel has rung under the water every night, though its rope rotted away forty years ago.
- Two punt-boys went out on Tuesday to look. Kit Hollin came back. Davy Sallow did not.
- [[Marrow Pell]] pays to know what is ringing it and to have it stop. The punts have stopped going out after dark, and the Ferryhouse is half empty.
- [[Sister Wren]] of [[The Reedwives]] wants the bell left hanging and ringable for the new moon. [[Old Ma Tessaly]] remembers the night the chapel sank.

## GM Notes

### The truth at the chapel

These are the working truths the places below use. Each is confirmed or changed under Open Questions.

- The ringer is [[The Sexton]], Abel Crane, the parish sexton who drowned the night the chapel sank. He is a wight. He does not know he is dead.
- He rings the old flood peal, three strokes and a pause, three strokes and a pause. It is a warning: get to the causeway, the water is coming. He tried to ring it forty years ago and the rope was cut. He has rung it every night since the drowned started to stir this autumn.
- He dives to the bell each night and strikes it with his bell-hammer, then climbs back to the dry spire loft.
- Davy Sallow is alive in the spire loft. The Sexton pulled him from the water, keeps him "above the flood", and feeds him raw eels. Davy is cold, hungry and too scared to swim back.
- The Sexton stops ringing for good when he believes the parish is out of the water. Then he rests, and his body sinks.
- The bell still works after he rests. If it hangs with its clapper at the new moon, the Reedwives can ring it. Ringing it the coven's way wakes the drowned (Reedwives GM truth, [[The Reedwives]] note).

### What counts as done

Stated by [[Marrow Pell]] in the briefing:

- **Full pay:** the crew can say what rings the bell, and the ringing has stopped.
- **Partial:** the crew can say what rings it but it still rings. Pell pays part (amount: Open Questions).
- **Bringing Davy home** is not part of Pell's pay. The Sallow family pays in eels and goodwill at the Market Stair.
- The job ends at the Ferryhouse debrief whether the Sexton rests, is destroyed, or still rings.

### Ways to stop the ringing

| Way | How | What it costs or changes |
|---|---|---|
| Lay him to rest | Convince the Sexton the parish is safe: proof the town stands (bring him to see the lights of the Market Stair, Davy saying it, a hymn or name from [[Old Ma Tessaly]]). DC 13 Persuasion with proof, DC 18 without. A cleric's prayer for the dead gives advantage. | No fight. The bell still hangs, ringable at the new moon. |
| Take the clapper | Dive down the tower shaft to the bell, about 15 feet under. DC 13 Athletics to reach it, then DC 14 Strength or DC 13 thieves' tools to free the rusted clapper pin. | Ends the ringing for good, and the Reedwives cannot ring the bell at the new moon. The Sexton fights anyone at the bell unless he is at rest or already convinced. |
| Destroy him | The main fight in the tower. | The ringing stops. The bell still hangs. |

|---|---|
| prays, heals or knows rites | The Sexton's rest; the parish dead; Turn Undead in the fight |

| reads, studies or casts | The peal board; the flood peal's meaning; Sister Wren's charms |
| talks, bargains or reads people | Sister Wren's price and her lie; Ma Tessaly's secret; the Sexton himself |

### Rumors

Three, one line each. A rumor heard in play is logged and changes nothing tonight.

- "Dace has sent to the city for six more blades, and you don't hire six blades to mind a gate." A carter drinking at the Ferryhouse. [[The Tollgate Company]], the Old Causeway.
- "Wren's been asking up and down the stalls who can get her a diving bell, and she's paying in old silver." A stallholder at the Market Stair, or [[Old Ma Tessaly]] if asked about the coven. [[The Reedwives]], the Market Stair.
- "That ledger clerk of the Company's, the one who won't wake? He talks about water coming under the door." A Company man at the Ferryhouse, off duty and nervous. [[The Tollgate Company]], the Tollgate.

### Route

- The Ferryhouse (Safe) to the Market Stair (Quiet) on foot. No roll.
- The Market Stair to the Reedwater (Dangerous) by punt. Roll a d6 on the Dangerous line of [[Map of the Reach]] going out.
- The Reedwater to the Drowned Chapel (Deadly). Roll a d6 on the Deadly line going out.
- Homeward: no rolls.
- Two dangerous stretches, so one roll each is the most this route asks for.

### Session load

- One briefing, one social and clue place (the Market Stair), one travel leg with up to two rolls, one exploration place with the main fight or the parley, the debrief.
- That fits 3 to 4 hours. If it runs long, cut in this order: skip the Reedwater roll; have Kit Hollin offer the punt himself; start the crew at the chapel at dusk.

**Main fight**
