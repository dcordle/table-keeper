# The Bell Under the Water: the fight in the Drowned Chapel

*DRAFT, for you to confirm. Built for five characters at level 4, per your Table-Keeper profile, with monster hit points as written.*

One call I made without asking: the missing punt-boy is Ma Tessaly's grandson, Wat, who punts for the Reedwives. It ties her want and the coven to the job and costs nothing if you'd rather he were a stranger. Swap the name and drop his Reedwives line if so.

## The Drowned Belfry

**Type:** Combat, with a rescue and a talk-down built in.

**Agenda:** Does the crew stop the bell and get Wat out alive, and how much of the drowned parish wakes while they try?

**Opening (read aloud):**
The punt bumps the belfry's broken louvres, and the only light is your lantern on black water inside. Pale shapes stand waist-deep in that water in rows, faces turned toward a square hole in the floor, as if they were waiting for a sermon. From somewhere under your feet, slow and heavy, the bell rings once.

### What is true when the crew arrives

It is night; the bell only rings after dark. The chapel sank forty years ago. The belfry's ringing chamber now sits at the waterline and the bell hangs in the flooded room below it.

- **The Sexton (Wight).** He drowned the night the chapel sank, while ringing the alarm to get the parish out. The rope was cut while he rang it. He has risen and is still ringing that alarm, standing on the bell frame 15 feet down and striking the bell by hand. He thinks the parish is still in danger. He wants the bell rung until everyone is out. He will talk, in old-fashioned plain speech, to anyone who talks to him before they hurt him.
- **The drowned parish (6 Zombies).** Woken by the ringing. Before the crew acts they just stand in the water facing the well. Once anyone living moves among them, or the Sexton calls them, they shuffle in to drag the living down.
- **The Big Drowned (Ogre Zombie).** A huge bloated eel-man, swollen with forty years of water. He stands at the foot of the spiral stair and blocks the way down.
- **The Hungry One (Ghast).** Not part of the parish. It came up the river for the dead and found something better: Wat, clinging to the bell-frame beam 10 feet above the floor. It is trying to reach him and stinks the whole room out.
- **Wat (commoner, 4 HP left, frightened).** Ma Tessaly's grandson. He and his friend came out on Tuesday because Sister Wren paid them to measure the tower for a rope. His friend got back to the punt. Wat didn't.

### The room

The ringing chamber is about 30 feet square.

- **The water.** Knee- to waist-deep everywhere: difficult terrain for everyone not undead or Small. The rotten floor is mostly holding.
- **The well.** A 10-foot square hole in the middle where the bell rope used to run. Below it is 15 feet of black water down to the bell. The Sexton is down there.
- **The beam.** An oak bell-frame beam crosses the room 10 feet up. Wat is on it. Climbing up is an Athletics check, DC 12. It holds one adult; a second creature on it makes it crack (see Complications).
- **The spiral stair.** In the north-west corner, going down into the water. The Big Drowned stands at its foot.
- **The louvres.** East wall, where the punt is moored. The crew enters here, and it is the only way out.

### The bell (homebrew, labelled)

The Sexton strikes the bell as a **bonus action** on each of his turns while he is on the bell frame. That is not in the Wight's stat block, so treat it as added. Each strike rings through the chapel. At the end of that round, **two more Zombies** climb out of the well. This can happen **twice at most**, for 4 extra Zombies. After that the bell has woken everyone close enough to come.

The ringing stops if any of these happen:

1. **The Sexton is destroyed, or he is pulled up off the bell frame.** A grapple works, and so does a Turn Undead that sends him fleeing up, among other things.
2. **The bell is silenced.** To do this, a character dives 15 feet down the well; swimming costs an extra foot per foot of movement. Then, as an action, they wrap the clapper in cloth or rope (Sleight of Hand or Athletics, DC 12) or cut its rotten leather strap (AC 11, 5 HP) so the clapper drops into the deep. A *Silence* spell cast on the bell also works.
3. **The Sexton is talked down.** He has to be told the parish got out. An Insight check, DC 12, shows he is warning, not hunting. Persuasion or Religion, DC 14, convinces him. Give advantage if they name a living parishioner (Ma Tessaly was a girl in that parish), bring something of hers, or have Wat call it to him. If he is convinced, he lets go of the bell and sinks.

**When the ringing stops,** the Zombies that the bell raised this fight sink back into the water at the end of that round. That covers the up-to-4 extras, not the 6 already standing. If the Sexton was talked down rather than destroyed, all the parish Zombies sink. The Ghast and the Big Drowned keep going either way.

### Stat blocks (SRD 5.2, hit points as written)

- **The Sexton (Wight, CR 3, 700 XP):** AC 14, HP 82, Speed 30 ft. Two Necrotic Sword attacks (+4, 1d8+2 slashing plus 1d8 necrotic), or swap one for Life Drain (DC 13 CON, 1d8+2 necrotic; see the SRD block for its full effect). Resistant to necrotic. *Tactics:* stays on the bell and rings. He only comes up if someone dives at him or the bell goes quiet. Then he fights whoever silenced it.
- **The Big Drowned (Ogre Zombie, CR 2, 450 XP):** AC 8, HP 85, Speed 30 ft. Slam +6, 2d8+4 bludgeoning. Undead Fortitude. *Tactics:* holds the stair. He hits whoever is closest and never pursues past the well.
- **The Hungry One (Ghast, CR 2, 450 XP):** AC 13, HP 36, Speed 30 ft. Stench, DC 10 CON. Claw +5, 2d6+3, DC 10 CON or Paralyzed for 1 turn. Bite +5, 1d8+3 piercing plus 2d8 necrotic. *Tactics:* goes for Wat first. It climbs at half speed and reaches the beam in round 2 unless someone gets in its way. If blocked, it claws to paralyze and then bites the paralyzed target. It flees out the louvres below 10 HP; it is the only one here that runs.
- **The drowned parish (6 Zombies at start, up to 4 more; CR 1/4, 50 XP each):** AC 8, HP 15, Speed 20 ft. Slam +3, 1d8+1. Undead Fortitude: radiant damage and critical hits get past it. *Tactics:* they crowd and flank. Your house rule gives +2, not advantage, and it works for them too. They push toward the well, as if trying to bring the living to the service.

### Complications

- **Round 2, Wat slips.** He drops to hanging by his hands. Someone must reach him or catch him this round. Otherwise he falls into the water at the Ghast's feet.
- **The beam cracks** when a second creature climbs on, whether it's the Ghast or a rescuer. At the end of the next turn it gives way, dropping everyone on it into the water. Each takes 1d6 bludgeoning and lands prone.
- **The floor goes.** The first time the Big Drowned hits the floor, or anyone is knocked prone near the well, a 5-foot section by the well collapses. Anyone standing there makes a DC 12 Dexterity save or drops into the bell room with the Sexton.

### Rules assumptions

Under the water, characters without a swim speed have disadvantage on melee attacks unless they use a dagger, javelin, shortsword, spear or trident, and ranged attacks are very limited. The undead don't breathe and simply walk the bottom. The crew needs their own light; everything here has darkvision.

### Outcomes

- **The bell stopped and Wat saved, with the Sexton destroyed or talked down.** The job is done. Marrow Pell pays, and the crew can tell him what was ringing it. Wat goes home to his grandmother. That's your hopeful beat, and it plays at the Ferryhouse debrief.
- **The bell silenced, Wat grabbed, and the crew pulls out by punt without finishing anyone.** This is the non-combat ending. It also counts as the job done. The bell has stopped; the drowned are only standing in the dark again.
- **The crew withdraws with the bell still ringing.** The job fails, but they know what is ringing it: a drowned sexton, still sounding the alarm. Pell pays half, or nothing; your call.
- **Wat is lost.** Ma Tessaly will not speak to the crew. Everything else plays as above.

### Connections (triggers for you to apply after the session, not now)

- **Wat's line** ("Sister Wren paid us to measure the tower for a rope") ties the job to the Reedwives' rope purchase from Session 2. It shows the coven is planning to come out here. It's a clue the crew can carry home, and it makes sense on its own.
- **Trigger, the clapper is gone.** It qualifies if the crew cut the clapper loose or carried it off. The change: the Reedwives cannot ring the bell at the new moon until they find a new clapper, so their "take the chapel" step stalls. It shows up when Sister Wren asks around the Market Stair for a bell-founder or a diver. It fires once.
- **Trigger, the Sexton is talked down.** If he was told the parish got out, he is at rest. A later bell-ringing raises only the parish, without him. That one fires once too.

## Budget check

- **Budget for 5 characters at level 4:** Moderate is 1,875 XP and High is 2,500 XP.
- **At the start:** Wight 700, Ogre Zombie 450, Ghast 450 and 6 Zombies at 300 come to **1,900 XP**. That is just above Moderate.
- **If the bell rings both times:** add 4 Zombies at 200 for **2,100 XP**. That is still under High.

So the fight sits in the band from the first round. It climbs toward High only if the crew lets the bell keep ringing, and they can earn it back down by silencing the bell.

**At level 3:** High for five is 2,000 XP. The start is right at that ceiling, and two tolls take it past. That's a real threat for a level 3 crew, so the bell matters more. **At level 5:** Low for five is 2,500 XP, so this is under Low and mostly a speed bump. The rescue and the Sexton carry the scene.

**Risk note.** Four players signed up, not five. I built it once for five and didn't rescale. For four characters at level 4, High is 2,000 XP: the start sits just under it, and two tolls push it past. It will feel hard. The swingiest piece is the Ghast. A failed DC 10 Con save leaves someone paralyzed, and then its bite is an automatic critical, about 30 damage. That can drop a level 4 wizard from full hit points. Watch it if it gets loose on Mira or another caster. The release valves already sit in the room: silencing the bell sends the extra Zombies back down, and the Ghast runs below 10 HP. A cleric's Turn Undead, if Teodor comes, swings it hard the other way. Most of these undead have Wisdom saves of +2 or lower.

**Time.** Run it at 45 to 60 minutes. Leave the last 20 minutes or so for the Ferryhouse debrief.
