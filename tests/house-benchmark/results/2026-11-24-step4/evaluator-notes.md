X is far more usable at the table: it gives read-aloud for each scene, structured NPC blocks with checks, a clue table, obstacle routes, a session-load check and a walkthrough of off-track crews, so a GM could run it as written. Y leaves almost all of the playable content as one-line "guess, confirm" items under Open Questions, has no read-aloud or NPC blocks, and keeps per-PC arc stages and spotlight hooks tied to named characters and Session 1 history. X still falls short in two places: the "how to stop it" fact has only one clue, and Nab's hidden fact (that he ran) has no stated way for the crew to get it.
